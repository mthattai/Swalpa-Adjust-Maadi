Swalpa adjust maadi - Bengalurina prema kathe

You play as the Red Power Co. or the Blue Water Co., both operating in a Bangalore neighbourhood. Each is trying to supply as many houses as possible, without much concern for how their actions affect the other. Meanwhile, roads are constantly dug up for Metro work, VIP visits, and floods, wreaking havoc on the infrastructure. The city's residents are left to deal with unpredictable power and water, and must suffer in silence.

A. Components

1 board, 6x6 spaces.
A deck of 8 card types: 2 Straight, 4 Bend, 2 Roadworks.

B. The board

Two opposite edges belong to Red's power supply, the other two belong to Blue's water supply. Any space along an edge with a connection reaching it is directly supplied.

C. The deck

You can see 8 upcoming cards from the deck, with the rightmost active card highlighted by a dashed orange border. Each card is one of the following:

Straight: red and blue connectors, perpendicular to each other.
Bend: red and blue L-shaped connectors, on opposite corners.
Roadworks.

Cards are dealt with fixed orientation.

D. The connection rule

When connectors of the same colour meet across an edge separating two cards, the corresponding utility (power or water) can flow across it in either direction. Every red or blue path connected all the way back to its supply is a live connection.

E. Setup

Fill the board with a random mix of Straight and Bend cards, with no Roadworks at setup. Check the board once: any space already connected to both supplies becomes a house immediately.

F. Houses

Houses are built when a space first connects to both red and blue supply. The player on whose turn this happens henceforth controls the space beneath the house (indicated by red or blue shading). A house built at setup stays neutral for the rest of the game. A house is permanent once created, even if it is later disconnected from supply. Roadworks can never strike a space that's already a house.

G. Your turn

Play alternates, Red first.

Draw a card.

If it's Roadworks: the card lands on a random space, you can't change it.

If it's a Straight or Bend: choose any space not controlled by the other player, and place the card there, in its printed orientation.

You can try out many spaces and see the resulting impact on the power and water flows. Once you are ready to commit, click the active card.

H. Revenue

After every turn, both players earn one point for every house currently connected to their own colour.

I. Winning

After 20 rounds, whoever has the higher revenue wins. Equal revenue is a tie.

J. Notes

Building houses early is important, because it gives you control of the underlying space. A house connected to both red and blue is neutral, since it earns revenue for both players. A player can gain by disconnecting a house from the opponent's supply, or reconnecting a house to their own.
